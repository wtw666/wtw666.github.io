From computer science to business

An algorithmic mindset shaped by CS and competitive programming

My path to business research began in computer science, where I built a strong foundation in algorithms, data structures, programming, and artificial intelligence. Experience in ACM/ICPC competitive programming sharpened my ability to recognize structure, reason under constraints, and translate ideas into efficient implementations.

Today, I bring that algorithmic mindset to revenue management and operational decision-making at the intersection of CS/AI and business: technically rigorous methods, computationally useful solutions, and consequential real-world decisions.
