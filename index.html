Background

Computer science and operations

My path to business research began in computer science, with a foundation in algorithms, data structures, programming, and artificial intelligence. Experience in ACM/ICPC competitive programming sharpened how I recognize structure, reason under constraints, and translate ideas into efficient implementations.

Today, I bring that algorithmic perspective to revenue management and operational decision-making: technically rigorous methods, computationally useful solutions, and consequential real-world decisions.
